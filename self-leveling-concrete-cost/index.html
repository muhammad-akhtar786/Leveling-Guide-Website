Self-Leveling Concrete Cost Guide

Editorial TeamLast updated September 2026
Quick answer

Self-leveling concrete bags commonly range from roughly $20–$50+ depending on bag size, brand, and formulation, with material cost per square foot depending heavily on pour depth and product coverage rate. Use the cost calculator with your product's actual price and coverage for an accurate estimate.

What Affects the Price

Price varies by brand, bag weight, rated depth capability, and any special formulation (fast-set, high-build, or fiber-reinforced products typically cost more than standard-duty compounds). Regional pricing and retailer also affect the final number, so treat any figure here as a planning estimate rather than a quote.

Typical Bag Price Ranges

Bag prices commonly fall in the $20–$50+ range at most home improvement retailers, with specialty or high-build products sometimes priced higher. Always check current pricing at the retailer you plan to buy from, since prices change and vary by region.

Estimating Cost Per Square Foot

Cost per square foot depends on your pour depth and the product's coverage rate at that depth — a thicker pour uses proportionally more material per square foot. The cost calculator on this site lets you enter your area, depth, bag price, and coverage rate to get a project-specific estimate rather than relying on an average.

DIY Material Cost vs. Hiring a Pro

A DIY pour's material cost is only part of the picture — tools (gauge rake, spiked roller, mixing paddle) are either a one-time purchase or a rental cost, and primer is a separate line item. Hiring a flooring contractor to handle the pour adds labor cost but can reduce the risk of a failed pour on a large or high-stakes job.

Budgeting Tips

  • Buy slightly more material than the calculator estimates to cover an unexpected low spot.
  • Price primer and tools separately — they're not included in per-bag pricing.
  • Compare coverage rate, not just bag price, since a cheaper bag with lower coverage can cost more per square foot.
  • Get current local pricing before finalizing a budget, since costs shift with material and fuel prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is self-leveling concrete expensive compared to other floor prep methods?

It's generally more expensive per square foot than a simple patch compound but is often more time- and cost-efficient than extensive manual floor grinding or building up a plywood subfloor, especially over larger uneven areas.

Does a thicker pour cost proportionally more?

Roughly yes — material cost scales with volume, so doubling the depth roughly doubles the material needed for the same area, though bag pricing and coverage rates mean the relationship isn't perfectly linear.
